# Model V3 implementation decisions

Machine timestamps, generation events, fitting counts, cutoffs and outcomes are in `run/decisions.jsonl`. This document explains the reasoning. The first candidate's complete logs and failed stress result remain in `../eight_class_v4/`; that is a historical working-folder name, not the user-facing model name.

1. **Preserve prior work.** Original V2 and the earlier precision-first review policy remain unchanged. Their hashes are recorded before fitting and checked afterward. No remote push or deployment is part of this work.
2. **Use the name Model V3.** The final candidate lives under `model_v3/`. Do not call the earlier ranking-only policy a newly trained V3 classifier.
3. **Add two scenario outputs.** The multiclass objective has eight classes: normal plus seven attack families. Both added families receive actual fitting examples; they no longer qualify as unseen-family tests.
4. **Keep the 40-input architecture stable.** Four IF domain scores, 32 direct behavior measurements and eight total engineered risk/context fields reproduce the existing contract. Eight omitted direct features still affect IF scores. Their supposed redundancy is an unverified assumption, not a demonstrated optimal selection.
5. **Do not confuse campaign and behavior.** The generator's kill chain contains credential dump, reconnaissance, staging and cloud-exfiltration phases. A daily campaign label can span several intended phases. An audit of all four fitting cohorts finds 83 kill-chain host-days, with 51 spanning multiple intended phases. All targets remain separate from predictors.
6. **Add independent binary behavior heads.** They allow concurrent behavior outputs and use phase-derived targets. They do not automatically mark every kill-chain day as every primitive, equate staging with hoarding, or prove a complete ordered campaign. Each label has its own calibration cutoff; failed labels abstain.
7. **Retain the first failed candidate.** Its standard queue had 15 true attack hosts and zero normal reviews, but its clustered-normal stress queue had 11 attack hosts and nine normal reviews. That stress outcome became development evidence for the follow-up candidate; it is not presented as untouched final validation of the final candidate.
8. **Represent sustained normal activity in fitting.** The robustness candidate combines standard seeds 66001/66002 with clustered-normal seeds 66041/66042: 51,840 host-days. Class targets, attack severity and final examples are not edited to raise metrics.
9. **Refit temporal learning correctly.** New XGBoost probabilities change the fused risks. Refit normal-only Ridge heads from chronological out-of-fold upstream scores, with no gaps, attack days or fold-boundary bridges. Keep seven inputs, three frozen LSTM64 layers and the existing strong regularization.
10. **Calibrate without final outcomes.** Separate standard and clustered-normal seeds 66611–66614 choose the daily thresholds and precision-first cutoff. Both model and rules freeze before seeds 66621/66622 are generated. The behavior supplement has its own freeze before seeds 66631/66632.
11. **Test an independent novelty path.** Candidate evidence is the largest daily second-largest domain anomaly score. It does not need a high classifier score. It must add true review candidates while passing prespecified false-review/precision gates. The robustness candidate's route fails the gate and remains disabled; structural implementation is not claimed as proven unseen-attack detection.
12. **Keep different ranking objectives visible.** Export faithful RedRVFL-error rankings, forced pair-probability rankings and thresholded up-to-K review queues separately. Compare equal review counts as well as K=5/10/15/20/30. Never rename accepted-queue precision as Precision@20.
13. **Fail closed on input/coverage problems.** Serving validates schema/order, keys, finite/nonnegative values, ratios, count subsets, binary weekend flags, daily durations, positive review budgets and model hashes. Missing completeness metadata prevents review eligibility. Labels, campaign IDs and synthetic maintenance flags cannot influence predictors.
14. **Monitor descriptively.** Quantile-bin training references expose per-feature distribution shifts. No arbitrary PSI threshold is marketed as a validated production alarm, and monitoring does not automatically alter model weights or decisions.
15. **Export actual explanations.** Exact per-booster TreeSHAP contributions reconstruct class margins. They do not sum to the averaged probability and do not by themselves explain a temporal score, campaign intent or a final human verdict.
16. **Do the educational walkthrough last.** Ten users × ten days means 100 records, seven warmup observations and three forecasts per user. Operational review correctly abstains because it needs 15 valid target days. A separate genuine 22-day context appendix includes all extra records rather than silently fabricating history. Complete split paths, parameters, gate states, readout products and SHAP contributions are supplied for the 100-row lesson.
17. **Do not claim near-production status from synthetic tests.** All candidates share a synthetic mechanism family, and phase labels describe intended injection. Report small-sample uncertainty, missed attacks, naming failures and disabled routes. An independent observed endpoint evaluation, deployment integration and campaign-level sequence validation remain required.

The first run encountered a Windows console encoding error after generating a cohort. UTF-8 output was enabled, the completed deterministic cohort was reused, and fitting restarted before any model selection. The error remains in the first candidate's log.

18. **Use observed evidence without inventing labels or history (21 October 2026).** Audit all 20 RealTime CSV feeds; preserve the source ZIP and all model weights. Confirm 6.6986 global days but at most three consecutive host calendar dates. Quarantine 286 host/date records from model admission because the daily40 collector contract, sensor coverage and timezone are unverified; preserve missingness instead of filling absent byte/LSASS measurements with zero. Add explicit declared-observed provenance and quality admission checks to `predict.py`. Retain legacy replay compatibility with an unverified-source summary status. Source gate tests and a retrospective synthetic sensor-loss experiment are recorded under `../realworld_evidence/run/`. Do not label this as a retrained model, blind real validation, or an accuracy improvement. The earlier ZIP remains the prior frozen distribution snapshot.
